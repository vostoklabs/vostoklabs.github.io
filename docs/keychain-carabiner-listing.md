# Keychain Carabiner Set

A snap-hook clip and a chain to hang your keys on. Any shape, any symbol, prints flat with no supports. Free, in your browser.

[Keychain Carabiner Set](https://vostoklabs.github.io/keychain-carabiner/) makes a clip that
actually snaps, a chain to go with it, and an optional charm — each part in whatever shape you
pick, with a symbol you drag onto the clip.

Try it out: https://vostoklabs.github.io/keychain-carabiner/ or click the image below

What's included, for free:

* Unlimited exports
* A snap-hook clip with a real gate: the whole ring flexes, so it clips on and holds
* Shapes for every part: teardrop, circle, heart, star, pear, squircle, hexagon, octagon, D-ring, shield, skull, a few seasonal ones, or upload your own outline
* A symbol on the clip, placed by dragging it on the model: clean silhouettes first, then hundreds of icons
* A chain two ways: open links you close by hand, in any shape — or Cuban-style links that print in place, already linked, grown straight out of the clip
* A print-in-place swivel on the clip, or a plain loop, or the chain straight through the clip
* An optional charm with a symbol cut through, engraved or raised
* Flat bevel or rounded edges on every part
* Up to 4 colours (hook, symbol, links, charm), each on its own filament slot in the exported 3MF
* An assembled view that hangs and swings as you move it, beside the print layout
* Four starter sets to begin from, undo and redo, and a share link that opens your exact set

What you need:
A 3D printer and filament. No glue, no hardware, no supports. Everything prints flat, face up,
exactly where it lands on the plate. PETG makes a springier gate than PLA.

It's totally free for personal use. If you want to sell physical prints made with this tool, you'll
need my Commercial License.
