# Fold-Up Box Generator

**Design a glue-free box, print it flat as one thin grooved sheet, fold it up by hand. Free, in your browser.**

[Fold-Up Box Generator](https://vostoklabs.github.io/foldbox/) turns a length, a width and a height
into a printable box net — the same flat blank a real carton is cut from, with the fold lines
already grooved in so it hinges instead of cracking.

Try it out: [https://vostoklabs.github.io/foldbox/](https://vostoklabs.github.io/foldbox/)
or click the image below

## What's included, for free

* Unlimited exports
* Four glue-free box structures — Mailer, Mailer with side flaps, Roll-end tray, Tray & telescoping lid
* Live fold animation — scrub it open and shut, fold a flat blank up or unfold a finished box back to the sheet
* Flat dieline view with panel names and the plate outline
* mm or inches, and your size can mean the inside or the outside of the box
* Carry handle, raised basket grips and hand holes in the ends
* A window on any face, with its own size and corner radius
* Hang tab — euro slot (ISO 15348) or round hole, on either end or both
* Lid depth, lid fit, tuck depth, tuck lock and thumb notch under Fine tuning
* Plate presets for A1 / P1 / X1, A1 mini and H2D
* Full control of sheet thickness, hinge thickness and groove width
* "Resize to fit" — one click sizes the box to your plate, or grows it as large as it will go
* Save and reload your projects as a file

## What you need

A 3D printer and one spool of filament. **No glue, no tape, no hardware, no supports.** Every
structure locks itself shut — the ends roll down over the corner ears and push two tabs through
the floor.

## What it does

* Set L × W × H, pick a structure, get a printable box
* Prints flat as a single sheet, about 0.40 mm thick — which is what 300 gsm cardstock measures, so it folds like card
* Fold lines print as grooves down to one layer, so they actually hinge
* Live 3D preview updates as you drag a slider, and folds itself up so you can see the box before you print it
* Exports a 3MF with the layer height, first-layer height and infill direction already set — the hinge is the first layer, and a stock profile would ruin it
* Every structure is a real trade dieline and carries the ECMA code it is built from, shown on screen
* Tells you continuously whether the blank fits your plate, instead of letting you find out at export
* Nothing leaves your device. Everything runs client-side, and the generator works fully offline
