# Pen Topper Generator

Put any name on any pen. Print it flat, no supports, snap it on. Free, in your browser.

[Pen Topper Generator](https://vostoklabs.github.io/pen-topper/) turns a name or a symbol into a
snap-on topper with a socket sized to the pen you already own.

Try it out: https://vostoklabs.github.io/pen-topper/ or click the image below

What's included, for free:

* Unlimited exports
* Any name, plus a second line
* 152 fonts, or import your own .ttf / .otf
* 1392 symbols, searchable, with the popular ones one click away
* 9 pen presets: hex pencil, BIC, round pen, Sharpie, stylus and Apple Pencil, broad marker, drinking and bubble tea straws, drawstring cord, or type your own diameter
* Round or hex hole, and three places to put it: inside the name, straight through, or a separate collar
* Fit dialled by grip ribs: snug, normal, loose, or none
* Three ways it sits: in line, on the corner, or upright, with pen angle and position sliders behind them
* Raised or engraved letters, on an outline, a block, or no plate at all
* 1, 2 or 3 colours, each on its own filament slot in the exported 3MF
* AMS or manual swap, with the pause heights snapped to whole layers
* Make a set: up to 100 names at once, laid out on plates, all from the design you just tuned
* Copy a link to a design, or save and reload your projects as a file

What you need:
A 3D printer, filament, and the pen you want it to fit. No glue, no hardware, no supports. It
prints flat and face up, and the ribs inside the bore are what grip the pen.

It's totally free for personal use. If you want to sell physical prints made with this tool, you'll
need my Commercial License.
