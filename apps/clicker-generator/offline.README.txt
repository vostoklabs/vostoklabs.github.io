Image to Clicker — offline copy
===============================

Unzip anywhere and double-click clicker-generator-offline.html. That is the whole
thing: one file, no install, no server, no internet. It works on a plane.

It is a big file (about 45 MB) because everything is inside it — the geometry
engine, the switch and block bodies, every sample picture and all 28 text fonts.
Give it a few seconds the first time your browser opens it. After that it is quick.

Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all run it. If your browser asks whether to allow
local files, say yes — that is the page reading itself, not the network.

WHAT IT DOES
  Drop in an image, an SVG or some text and it traces the outline, thickens it into
  a printable body and gives you a fidget clicker, a keycap or a magnet. There are
  six sample images built in if you just want to see it work.

  "Download 3MF" gives you a Bambu Studio project with the colours already assigned
  to filament slots. It lands in your normal downloads folder.

PRINTING IT
  The 3MF opens straight into Bambu Studio or Orca with the plate laid out. No
  supports. If you have an AMS the colours are already separated; if you do not,
  the slicer will ask you to pause and swap.
