Fold-Up Box Generator — offline copy
=====================================

Unzip anywhere and double-click foldbox-offline.html. That is the whole thing:
one file, no install, no server, no internet. It works on a plane.

Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all run it. If your browser asks whether to
allow local files, say yes — that is the page reading itself, not the network.

WHAT IT MAKES
  Cut files (.zip)   SVG + DXF + an assembly sheet, for a laser, a blade cutter
                     or a printer and a craft knife.
  Printable (.3mf)   the same net as a 0.4 mm sheet you 3D print flat and fold
                     once. Slice at 0.2 mm, no supports.

Downloads land in your normal downloads folder.

BEFORE YOU CUT ANYTHING
  Every cut file carries a 100 mm rectangle on the ENGRAVE layer. Measure it in
  your cutting software first. If it reads 133 mm or 75 mm, the importer guessed
  the wrong DPI — use the DXF, which carries its units inside the file.

  If you are cutting card, type in how thick your card ACTUALLY is. Every tab,
  slot and lid clearance is built from that one number, and "300 gsm" is anywhere
  from 0.30 to 0.46 mm. Stack ten sheets, measure, divide by ten.
