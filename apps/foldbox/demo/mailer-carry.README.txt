Mailer box 64x60x30
===================

Mailer box — 15 panels, 14 folds, no glue
Generated by Vostok Labs — https://vostoklabs.github.io

CUT THIS
  Stock              Cardstock 300 gsm (16 pt)
  Measured caliper   0.38 mm   <- every panel was built for THIS number
  Sheet              A4 landscape (297 × 210)
  Blank size         192 x 211 mm
  Machine            Bambu H2D — blade + pen
  Fold lines         drawn (a pen line you fold by hand)

THE FILE
  Colours and layers are the operation. Nothing relies on line style:
    #FF0000  CUT      cut all the way through
    #0000FF  CREASE   fold line — score or draw, never cut through
    #000000  ENGRAVE  the 100 mm check rectangle (see below)

  CHECK THE SCALE BEFORE YOU CUT. The rectangle on the ENGRAVE layer is exactly
  100 mm wide. Measure it in your cutting software. If it reads 133 mm or 75 mm,
  your importer has guessed the wrong DPI — use the DXF instead, which carries
  its units inside the file.

IN YOUR SOFTWARE
  Basic cut for the outline, Drawing line for the fold marks — one plate, one pen swap, and the machine pauses for it. No scorch. Bambu Suite has no crease operation, so the folds are pen marks you fold by hand.
  Select everything and use ATTACH before assigning processes. Unattached objects
  get sorted onto different plates and the folds lose their registration.

ASSEMBLY
  1. Crease all 14 fold lines before you fold anything: lay a ruler along each one and run it over, then open it flat again. Card creased first folds square; card folded cold wanders.
  2. Stand the front and back walls up, then fold the four corner ears inward so they lie flat against the ends.
  3. Now roll each end over the ears: the wall up, the narrow strip across the top, the inner panel straight back down inside.
  4. Push the two tabs on each inner panel through the slots in the floor. That is the whole lock — the ends are now double-walled and nothing can spring open.
  5. Fold the lid over and slide its tuck down inside the front wall.

ABOUT THE FIT
  Caliper is the number everything here depends on, and the number on the packet
  is not it — 300 gsm card is anywhere from 0.30 to 0.46 mm depending on its bulk.
  If your box is tight or loose, measure a stack of ten sheets with calipers,
  divide by ten, type that in and re-export.
