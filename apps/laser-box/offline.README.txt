Laser Box — offline copy
========================

Unzip anywhere and double-click laser-box-offline.html. That is the whole thing: one
file, no install, no server, no internet. It works on a plane.

Everything is inside it — the geometry engine, the patterns and the ready-made boxes —
so give it a few seconds the first time your browser opens it. After that it is quick.

Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all run it. If your browser asks whether to allow local
files, say yes — that is the page reading itself, not the network.

WHAT IT DOES
  Finger-jointed boxes for a laser cutter: an open tray, a lift-off lid, a hinged lid
  that latches shut, or a chest of one to three drawers. Pick a box on the right — or
  start from a ready-made one under the box types — set its size, and put a pattern on
  the sides you choose.

  "3D Preview" shows the box assembled. "2D Design" names every piece. "Export Preview"
  is the cut file exactly as it downloads.

  "Download SVG" gives you the cut file in millimetres: red lines cut, blue lines score,
  black areas engrave. The kerf is already in it, so set your laser software's own
  offset to 0. "Save" writes a small .json of your settings that "Load" reads back.

CUTTING IT
  Measure your sheet with calipers and type its thickness under Material. Cut one corner
  first: if it falls apart, move Fit one step tighter; if it will not go together, one
  step looser. Help shows how to put the box you designed together, step by step.
