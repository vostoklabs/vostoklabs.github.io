Keychain Carabiner Set — offline copy
=====================================

Unzip anywhere and double-click keychain-carabiner-offline.html. That is the whole
thing: one file, no install, no server, no internet. It works on a plane.

It is about 4 MB because everything is inside it — the geometry engine, the symbol
font and the shape library. Give it a few seconds the first time your browser opens
it. After that it is quick.

Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all run it. If your browser asks whether to allow
local files, say yes — that is the page reading itself, not the network.

WHAT IT DOES
  A snap-hook clip and a chain to hang something on. Pick a shape for the hook, a
  shape for the links, drag a symbol onto the hook, add a charm if you want one.
  Every part prints flat, face up, exactly where it lands on the plate — no supports.

  "Assembled" shows the set hanging as worn. "Print layout" shows what you download.

  "Download 3MF" gives you a project with the plate already laid out. It lands in
  your normal downloads folder. "Save" writes a small .json of your settings that
  "Load" reads back, so a design you liked is not gone when you close the tab.

PRINTING IT
  The gate is the wavy cut in the hook. The whole ring is the spring: push the two
  halves apart to clip it on. Firm is snappier; Soft is safer on a printer that
  over-extrudes. PETG makes a springier gate than PLA.

  Open links and connector rings have one slit each. Flex one open, thread the next
  through, let it spring shut.

  "Print in place" grows the chain out of the hook instead, already threaded. Print
  it flat as it lands and flex the links free. A first layer squished too wide is
  what fuses them, so if they stick, lower the first-layer flow a little.
